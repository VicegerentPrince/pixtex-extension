Export the n8n workflow you're editing as a crisp PNG, PDF, SVG, JPEG or WebP image, rendered from the workflow itself rather than screenshotted.

Pixtex adds one Export button to the n8n editor. Click it and the whole workflow is saved as an image: every node, however large the workflow and however little of it fits on your screen. No stitching screenshots together, no cropping, no editor buttons in the picture.

WHAT YOU GET
• The whole workflow in one image, sharp at up to 4× (6× and 8× with Pixtex Pro)
• PNG, PDF, SVG, JPEG and WebP
• Four finished looks: Signature, Like n8n, Docs and Slide. Like n8n and Docs draw n8n's own node icons
• Diagram poster: the same workflow as a one-page flowchart that someone who has never opened n8n can follow
• AI agents with their model, memory and tools, sticky notes and named branches, drawn the way n8n draws them
• Open in Pixtex: hand the workflow to the full editor at pixtex.dev to restyle it before you export
• n8n Cloud and self-hosted n8n, versions 1.x and 2.x

HOW TO USE IT
On n8n Cloud, the Export button is already in the corner of the editor. On a self-hosted n8n, click the Pixtex toolbar button while your workflow is open, and choose "Just this time" or "Always on" for your n8n's address.

The arrow next to Export picks the format, the look and the size. Pixtex exports the workflow as n8n last saved it; a workflow that has never been saved can be copied in n8n (Ctrl+A, then Ctrl+C) and pasted into Pixtex instead.

WHAT HAPPENS TO YOUR WORKFLOW
Workflows can contain secrets, so this is exactly what the extension does:
• It reads a workflow only when you click Export, through the same n8n endpoint your editor uses, with your existing session.
• Before anything leaves the page, it removes credential references, pinned execution data, and owner and sharing details.
• The workflow is sent to api.pixtex.dev in the body of the request, rendered in memory and discarded. It is never put in a URL, never logged and never stored.
• Open in Pixtex passes the workflow to pixtex.dev inside your browser, once, within a minute, and never through a URL.
• The extension has no analytics and no tracking. Pixtex counts renders by the kind of app that asked for them: a number, not who.
